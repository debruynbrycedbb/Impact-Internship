What we collect

  • What you type in the form: your name, email address, school or university, study year and, if you add it, your WhatsApp number.
  • Your quiz answers and your match (the internship role and destination).
  • Whether you ticked the box to receive news from Impact Internship.

  • To contact you about those internships, by email or WhatsApp if you gave a number.
  • To send you news from Impact Internship, only if you ticked that box.
